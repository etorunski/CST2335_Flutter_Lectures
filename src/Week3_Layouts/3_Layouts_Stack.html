A Stack() widget is something that lays out items one over top of the other. For instance, a picture on the bottom, and some words over the picture:

Stack(
          children: <Widget>[
            Image.asset("images/algonquin.jpg"),
            Text("Algonquin College", style: TextStyle(fontSize: 30.0, backgroundColor:Colors.white ),)
          ])

Will give you this:

This shows our image, and then the text "Algonquin college" on top of the image, in the top left corner by default. There are 9 choices of where to align elements using a stack:

  • AlignmentDirectional.topStart - This is the top left corner
  • AlignmentDirectional.topCenter - This is the top middle
  • AlignmentDirectional.topEnd - This is the top right corner
  • AlignmentDirectional.centerStart - This is the middle left side
  • AlignmentDirectional.center - This is the middle
  • AlignmentDirectional.centerEnd - This is the middle right side
  • AlignmentDirectional.bottomStart - This is the bottom left corner
  • AlignmentDirectional.bottomCenter - This is the bottom middle
  • AlignmentDirectional.bottomEnd - This is the bottom right corner
